When to seek professional advice

Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making substantial dietary changes if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease, high cholesterol, food allergies or intolerances, take regular medication, or are managing a diagnosed condition. Infants, older adults and people with complex medical needs should follow personalised guidance.
