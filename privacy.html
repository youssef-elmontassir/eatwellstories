Privacy policy

This policy explains what information EatWellStories collects, how it is used and the choices available to readers.

1. Information we collect

  • Analytics data: anonymised page views, device type, rough region and referral source, used to understand which guides are useful. Analytics are configured to avoid identifying individual readers.
  • Newsletter data: the email address you submit for the free meal planner checklist, used only to send the newsletter and lead magnet; never sold or shared.
  • Contact messages: your name, email and message content, used to reply to your enquiry.

2. Cookies

The site may use minimal cookies or local storage for analytics and newsletter preferences. Necessary cookies support basic site function. You can block cookies in your browser settings; core articles remain readable.

3. Affiliate links and advertising

Future articles may contain Amazon Associates or similar affiliate links: we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. Display advertisements, when enabled, will be served by a network (for example Ezoic) and may set their own cookies governed by that provider's policy. Sponsored posts are clearly labelled.

4. External links

Outgoing links to WHO, Harvard, NHS, USDA, Cleveland Clinic and other references open in a new tab. We do not control third-party pages and recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the email address or message you submitted. Contact hello@eatwellstories.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

6. Children

The blog is intended for adult readers and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
